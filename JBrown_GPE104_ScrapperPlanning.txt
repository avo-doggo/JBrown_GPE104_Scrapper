SCRAPPER
Jameson Brown
Github: https://github.com/avo-doggo/JBrown_GPE104_Scrapper 


A game, where the player is a scrap collector, breaking into ships and collecting parts, while avoiding security drones and crew members.

The core gameplay loop is:
•	The player is deposited onto a spaceship.
•	The player must go through the ship and collect different scrap. Different types of scrap give different amounts of points.
•	There will be small, medium, and large scrap.
•	The player has to reach at least the designated amount of points for the level before they can escape the ship. 
•	Extra points collected after the required amount means a better score. There is  a  star system: 1 star,  2 star, 3 star.
•	The player, while collecting scrap, must avoid enemies: Drones and Crew.
•	Drones alert crew to player position, but do not cause a game over. Crew, if they catch they player, will cause a game over.
•	Once all scrap is collected, the player must return to the start without being caught.
•	Objectives example for stars:
o	Complete level: 1 Star
o	Reach X points: 2 Star
o	Do not get spotted by drones: 3 Star
